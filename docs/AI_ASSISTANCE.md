# AI assistance disclosure

AI-assisted tools were used to edit wording and draft analysis and figure code.
The authors remain responsible for the methods, claims, calculations, diagrams,
and citations. The manuscript includes the same general disclosure.

The revision requests asked for clearer language, verification of mathematical
and experimental claims, reproducible figures/tables, and reference checks.
Supporting text and publication metadata were checked against primary sources;
numeric claims were checked against saved scores and independent calculations
within the stated audit scope. These checks are not a guarantee of correctness.

## Available revision-prompt excerpts

The following author requests guided the assisted revision:

> Ensure all content, especially theoretical parts and experimental results,
> is verified, reproducible, and easily understandable

> Verify all mathematical equations and theoretical concepts

> Ensure all references are correctly formatted and verified through Google
> Scholar.

These are excerpts, not a complete prompt transcript. Publication metadata and
claim support were ultimately checked against original papers and official
publication records. AI-written wording or code was not treated as evidence.

No additional provider judgments were collected during this release revision.
The release uses the existing original panel, separate preference panel, and
completed ten-task repeat-call slice. Private meeting context and complete
assistant conversation history are not research data and are not published.
